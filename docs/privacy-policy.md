# CalmLoop Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

CalmLoop is built to be private by design. There are **no accounts, no user database, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking**. We don't sell or share data, and we don't collect it in the first place.
(This text matches the Privacy & Terms screen inside the app.)

## What is stored, and where

Everything you create stays **on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- journal entries and their summaries or reflection prompts
- breathing and grounding session history
- your preferred name and app settings
- a local count of taps on book links (never sent anywhere)

This data is excluded from Google cloud backups. It can move to a new phone only through a direct
device-to-device transfer that you start. **Settings → Delete All My Data** erases it, and
uninstalling the app removes it.

## Microphone and speech

Voice journaling and voice grounding use your phone's built-in speech recognition service to turn
speech into text. CalmLoop does not record or keep audio. Depending on your device, that service
(usually Google) may process audio under its own privacy policy. You can always type instead.

## AI reflections (optional, off by default)

If you turn on AI reflections:

- **What is sent:** the text of the journal entry you save, or, when you search, your search words and
  the text of your journal entries.
- **Where it goes:** over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to CalmLoop's relay server (hosted on
  Cloudflare), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API and returns the summary.
- **What is kept:** the relay stores and logs nothing. Google processes the request under the
  [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).
- **Why:** only to generate the summary or search result you asked for.

AI-generated content is labeled in the app and may be inaccurate. You can turn AI off at any time in
Settings; nothing you write then leaves your device. AI tools were also used to help build CalmLoop.

## Links, calls and texts

Crisis lines open your phone or messaging app, and book links open your browser. What happens there
is governed by those services. CalmLoop doesn't earn money from book links.

## Children

CalmLoop is intended for adults (18+) and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and show the notice in the app again.
